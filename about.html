What we do

Four things, in one company.

  1. Develop

    We underwrite sites and plan unit mix, drive lanes, and parking against the local market before anything is poured.

  2. Build

    Projects are delivered as operating stores. Nothing is listed for rent until it is genuinely open.

  3. Operate

    Leasing, listings, access, and day-to-day operations at every store we own. Renters transact online.

  4. Manage for others

    The same operating stack, run on someone else's store. That is Owners.

How we run a store

The standards we hold everywhere.

Access
Every open store is 24 hours a day, seven days a week. We do not set an open store back to office hours.
Renting
Rentals are completed online on each store's own site, so nobody waits for an office to open.
Listings
Name, address, phone, hours, and rent links are kept accurate on every store, because a wrong pin does not convert.
Construction
A site under construction is never marketed as open and never carries a rent link. See Development.
