Does the AI phone agent sound robotic?

It speaks in a natural voice, answers common questions about your business, and hands off or takes a message when a person is needed. Call our line to hear it.

Do you work with medical practices and HIPAA?

Yes. We build HIPAA-aware: patient data stays in your existing HIPAA-compliant booking, forms and records systems, never on the marketing site. There is no such thing as a HIPAA certification, and your practice and its vendors keep responsibility for compliance.

Yes. Start with one piece, such as AI phone answering or your Google listing, and add more later if it makes sense.
