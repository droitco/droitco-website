What we do

Five jobs. You keep the keys to the money.

  1. 01

    Answer people

    Texts, emails, and missed calls. Same day. We pick up as the manager.

  2. 02

    Collect late rent

    If someone has not paid, we text and email them. Sunday, Wednesday, and Friday at 9am CT.

  3. 03

    Turn the gate code off

    Five days late, their code stops working. They pay, we turn it back on. We do not open or close the actual gate.

  4. 04

    Empty units that never pay

    Two months with no payment, we start the legal notices so the unit can be emptied and rented again.

  5. 05

    All of it, plus a report

    Take every job above, and we send you a monthly report. You still approve refunds, lawyers, and anything that costs money.
