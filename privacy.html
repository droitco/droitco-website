This page does not describe every vendor system used at a store. Your facility lease and that store’s own notices also apply.

How we use it

SMS / text messaging

SMS / text messaging: If you opt in to texts from Droit, we may send account and rental messages to the mobile number you provide. We do not sell mobile opt-in or text-message consent. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers who help us run accounts, billing, or communications, or when required by law.
