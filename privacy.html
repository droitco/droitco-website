Privacy Policy

Droit LLC (Droitco / Smart Self Storage). Public privacy notice for this company site and for account and rental text messages.

Who we are

Droit LLC operates and manages self-storage and builds new storage sites.

Information we may have

If you contact us, rent a unit, or opt in to texts, we may have the name, phone number, email, and rental details you provide. Facility websites may collect information needed to quote, rent, or bill a unit.

This page does not describe every vendor system used at a store. Your facility lease and that store’s own notices also apply.

How we use it

  • To operate and manage storage accounts
  • To answer questions and provide rental service
  • To send account and rental messages if you opt in to SMS

SMS / text messaging

SMS / text messaging: If you opt in to texts from Smart Self Storage (Droit LLC), we may send account and rental messages to the mobile number you provide. Examples include move-in, billing, gate/access, and customer-service texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help or call (888) 711-6050. Consent is not a condition of renting. No mobile opt-in or text message consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell mobile opt-in or text-message consent. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers who help us run accounts, billing, or communications, or when required by law.
